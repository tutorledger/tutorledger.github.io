Questions

Before you buy.

Do I need Excel, or does Google Sheets work?

Both, and both are tested. You get one .xlsx file that opens directly in Excel and imports into Google Sheets with every formula, the conditional formatting and all three charts intact.

Every formula was deliberately restricted to the set both applications support — nothing newer than Excel 2007, no dynamic arrays, no Sheets-only functions. That restraint is the reason it survives the import instead of arriving full of #NAME?.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay $29 once and the file is yours permanently. There's nothing to cancel and no account to keep. Updates within the same version are free — you'll get an email with the new file.

Will it break if I insert a row or change something?

The three calculation sheets are protected, so you can't overwrite a formula by accident. The sheets you type into are ordinary rows — insert, sort and filter freely. Formulas are filled down in advance rather than generated dynamically, precisely so that editing doesn't disturb them.

I bill monthly tuition, not per session. Does it still work?

Yes. Each student is set to monthly, per-session, or package billing, and the system handles a mix across your roster. Monthly students are billed from the recurring schedule; per-session students are billed from their logged sessions.

Can I use it for a music, dance or language school?

Mostly, yes — the money side is identical: recurring tuition, families with siblings, no-shows, instructor pay. What it won't do is anything specific to those disciplines, such as recital or exam management. It was built for tutoring and it's honest about that.

Does my data go anywhere?

No. It's a spreadsheet file on your computer or in your own Google account. There is no server, no login, no sync, and no analytics inside the file. We never see a single student name.

How long does setup take?

About 20 minutes for your settings, tutors and rates, plus roughly a minute per student to enter your roster. The included guide walks through it in the order that avoids rework. After setup, budget about fifteen minutes a week.

How do I get it after paying?

Checkout runs through Payhip, which handles the payment and any applicable VAT or sales tax. Your download link appears immediately and is also emailed to you.

Are there reviews?

Not yet — TutorLedger is new, and we won't publish testimonials we don't have. That's exactly why the refund policy is unconditional and why the free calculator lets you test the thinking before you spend anything.
