Privacy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

The short version

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third-party servers. The profit calculator runs entirely in your browser — your figures are never transmitted anywhere. Your students' names never reach us at all, because the product is a file on your own computer.

1. Who is responsible

This site and product are operated under the trading name TutorLedger, by an independent developer. For any privacy question or request, email tutorledger.support@gmail.com. We answer every message and action any valid request free of charge.

Purchases are made through our shop on Etsy. Etsy operates the marketplace and processes the payment, and is an independent data controller for everything that happens on its platform — its own privacy policy covers that. If you need our full details in order to exercise a data-protection right, email us and we will provide them.

2. When you browse this site

We set no cookies and use no analytics, tag managers, advertising pixels, embedded fonts or third-party scripts. All styling and code is served from this site itself.

Our host, GitHub Pages, records standard server request logs including IP address, in line with GitHub's own privacy practices. We do not have access to those logs and cannot identify visitors from them.

3. When you use the profit calculator

Every figure you type is processed in your browser and discarded when you close the tab. Nothing is submitted, saved, or transmitted. There is no account and no sign-up.

4. If you submit your email address

The calculator has an optional form for emailing your results. If you use it, the address you type is processed by Formspree, our form provider, and forwarded to us.

We use it to send you the results you asked for and, occasionally, an update about the product. We do not sell, rent or share it. Every message includes an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time by emailing us.

Legal basis, where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies: your consent, given when you submit the form.

5. If you buy the product

Checkout happens on Etsy. Your payment card details are processed by Etsy Payments and its payment providers, and are never seen by, or transmitted to, us.

From a completed purchase we receive, through Etsy, your name, the delivery country used for tax purposes, the product bought, and any message you send us. Etsy may relay your email address or keep contact inside its own messaging system. We use this to deliver your download, send free updates within the same version, handle support, and process refunds. We keep purchase records for as long as required for tax and accounting purposes.

6. Your data inside the product

TutorLedger is a spreadsheet file that lives on your device or in your own Google account. Your students, guardians, invoices and payments stay entirely under your control. The file contains no tracking, no phone-home code and no network calls of any kind. We never see any of it.

Note that you are the data controller for the personal data of your students and their families. Handling it is your responsibility under the privacy law that applies to you.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict its processing. Email us and we will action any valid request, normally within 30 days and always free of charge.

If you are in the UK or EU and you think we have handled your data improperly, you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

8. Third parties we rely on

  • GitHub Pages — website hosting and server logs
  • Etsy — marketplace, checkout, payment processing, tax handling and file delivery
  • Formspree — optional email form on the calculator page

We use no other processors, and we add none without updating this page.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top of this page.
