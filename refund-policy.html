Refund Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

30 days. No questions. No forms.

Email tutorledger.support@gmail.com within 30 days of your purchase with the email address you used at checkout. We refund you in full. You don't have to explain why, delete the file, or reply to a single follow-up question.

How it works

  1. Email us within 30 days of purchase from, or quoting, the address you checked out with.
  2. We confirm and issue the refund through Paddle, normally within two business days.
  3. Your bank or card issuer returns the money, typically within 5–10 business days depending on your provider.

You will not receive a survey, a discount offer, or an attempt to talk you out of it.

Why the policy is this simple

TutorLedger is new and has no reviews yet. Asking someone to spend money on an unfamiliar product from an unfamiliar seller is a genuine risk, and the honest way to handle that is to carry the risk ourselves rather than ask you to.

If the system doesn't show you more than its own price in leaked revenue within a month, it hasn't earned what you paid for it.

Your statutory rights

This policy is offered voluntarily and sits on top of your legal rights — it does not replace them.

Consumers in the United Kingdom and European Union normally have a 14-day right to cancel a distance purchase. For downloadable digital content that right can end once the download begins and you have acknowledged it. Our 30-day policy is deliberately longer and less conditional than the statutory minimum, so in practice you can rely on it instead.

The one limit

We reserve the right to decline a refund where there is clear evidence of abuse — for example repeated purchase-and-refund cycles from the same buyer, or redistribution of the file in breach of the licence terms. This has nothing to do with disliking the product, changing your mind, or finding it wasn't right for your business. Those are all covered without question.

Chargebacks

Please email us before opening a dispute with your bank. A chargeback costs us a fee and takes months to resolve, while a refund request takes us about two minutes. We have never refused one.

Payments are handled by Paddle.com Market Ltd, the merchant of record for every sale. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
